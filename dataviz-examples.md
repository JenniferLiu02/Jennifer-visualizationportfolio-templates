# Data Visualization Projects

Explore my work in Telling Stories with Data at Carnegie Mellon University. Each project includes visualizations and an explanation of the design process.

## Visualizing Government Debt

An exploratory heatmap of OECD debt-to-GDP ratios from 1995 to 2019 and a redesigned bar chart comparing countries in 2019.

[View the charts and reflection](https://jenniferliu02.github.io/Jennifer-visualizationportfolio-templates/#assignment-visualizing-government-debt) · [Interactive heatmap](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/jennifer.liu4619/viz/OECDDebttoGDPRatios1995-2019_17889178234160/Sheet1) · [Interactive 2019 comparison](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/jennifer.liu4619/viz/OECDDebttoGDPRatios2019/Sheet12)

## Critique by Design: Bears Will Be Boys

A critique and redesign of a visualization about pronouns used for animal characters in children’s books. The project includes sketches, feedback from two readers, sources, and an interactive Tableau dashboard.

[Explore the critique and redesign](https://jenniferliu02.github.io/Jennifer-visualizationportfolio-templates/critique-by-design.html)

## Same Basket, Different City

A final data story comparing the estimated cost of the same eight-item grocery basket in Pittsburgh, New York, San Francisco, and Seattle.

[Read the finished Shorthand story](https://carnegiemellon.shorthandstories.com/same-basket-different-city/index.html)
